What sets you apart is changing

As the power to produce
answers spreads,
knowing what to ask
becomes your edge.

From the speed of answers to the depth of questions

From knowing a lot,
to asking better questions.

Knowledge built up through years of study still matters. Now AI draws on vast amounts of information to quickly suggest explanations and draft answers. Holding knowledge and reproducing the right answer is becoming less and less enough to stand out.

Instead of racing AI on speed, find the problem you want to solve and ask new questions. Why is that so? What is the evidence? Is there another possibility?

If you don’t ask,
you may stay inside the frame of the answer AI gives you.

  1. 01 / You ask

    Set the problem and direction.

    Find what you are curious about and the problem to solve, and put your purpose and conditions into the question.

  2. 02 / Answer with AI

    Explore answers and alternatives.

    Use AI to get a first draft, shift perspectives, and look at several possibilities.

  3. 03 / You judge again

    Review, then ask again.

    Check the evidence and errors, decide what fits your situation, and form your next question.

So that AI’s first answer
is not the end of your thinking.

    01What is Markdown?A way to mark the structure of text with simple symbols

    # is a heading, - is a list item. Markdown organizes writing with a few symbols instead of complex formatting. The structure of headings and lists can become the branches of a mind map.

    You don’t need to memorize it all now. Start by changing the example above little by little.

    Start with three symbols ↗
    02What is a mind map?A way to see how your thoughts connect at a glance

    A mind map is a map that connects related thoughts to a central topic like branches. It is not about drawing a pretty picture, but a tool for seeing what is connected and what is missing.

    Try it when you need to see several threads together, such as the packing list for a trip, the agenda and decisions of a meeting, or the concepts in a book.

    When is it useful? Read more ↗
    03Why EasyMindMap?Write it as text, see it as a map, keep thinking with AI

    You can organize your thinking by moving between documents and thought maps. Import Markdown documents as maps, export maps as text, and set up an AI connector to read and extend maps in the middle of a conversation.

    A single note is enough to start. As you get used to it, expand to AI integration and file export.
